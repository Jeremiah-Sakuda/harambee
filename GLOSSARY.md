# Harambee group booking

Harambee coordinates each participant’s agreement and payment for a shared trip.

## Language

**Plan version**:
A specific cabin, group and allocation of exact shares that participants review together.

**Consent**:
A participant’s agreement to their exact share in a particular plan version. Consent alone is not a payment authorization.

**Authorization**:
A buyer-approved hold for a participant’s share. The money has not yet been captured.

**Capture**:
A confirmed collection of previously authorized money.

**Recovery**:
The process of resolving uncertain payment outcomes, refunding confirmed captures and releasing remaining holds after a trip stops.

**Revision option**:
A proposed cabin and allocation for the remaining group, checked against current budgets and participants’ own statements before it can become a plan version.

**Limit confirmation**:
A participant’s explicit confirmation of a particular spending limit read from their own words. It does not replace consent to a plan version.

**Payment outcome**:
The known result of a payment operation, including whether it is still pending or uncertain. A pending or uncertain refund is not money already returned.
