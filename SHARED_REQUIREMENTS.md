# Shared hackathon requirements

The supplied competition brief requires meaningful PayPal and AI use, a functional judge-accessible build, an open-source repository/license, and a public demonstration video shorter than three minutes. The supplied deadline is November 12, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. EST, with judging access maintained through December 15. Recheck the [official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) before submission; these dates and requirements are planning input, not a fresh rules verification.

Implementation, design, impact, innovation, and presentation are equally weighted in the supplied rubric. Target first place overall without inventing evidence of transactions, adoption, or user outcomes.

Use sandbox money; distinguish genuine sandbox calls, local inventory, simulated failures, and recordings. Keep provider credentials on the server. Use USD integer cents, explicit personal approvals, stable idempotency keys, and reconciliation after uncertain outcomes. A refund request is not proof that funds have returned.

Harambee's complete local workflow is simulated. Its optional PayPal sandbox lab is separate from group booking, and optional AI must be configured to demonstrate actual model use. Completing those integration release gates, publishing the repository under the intended visibility, recording a public video, and maintaining hosted judge access remain submission work.
