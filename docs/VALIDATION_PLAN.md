# Validation protocol — not completed research

No participants have been recruited or observed and no merchant has been interviewed. Use consented synthetic trip notes; retain only anonymized research records.

## Organizers and group comprehension

Recruit five adults who organized a group trip recently. Ask how deposits were collected, how often somebody left, who fronted the cost, and what workarounds they used. Record concrete prior events, not agreement with a pitch.

Observe one group (at least three people) using separate participant views: save a ceiling, approve version 1, withdraw one person, publish a capped revision, review top-ups, and inspect a refund-pending scenario. Avoid instructions beyond the task. Record start/end, assisted steps, errors, and each person's explanation of held versus charged versus refund pending. Ask whether the capped split feels acceptable and whether they inferred anyone's ceiling from the final shares.

Blank record: `participant pseudonym | previous trip date | prior deposit problem | workaround | task | completion seconds | assisted steps | misunderstood states | fairness comment | evidence consent`.

## AI usefulness

Use the fixed 21-case corpus plus 12 consented real planning briefs. Counterbalance manual entry, local parser, and connected model order across participants. Preserve provider/model, output, source-grounding warning, changed fields, review time, and final participant-confirmed ceiling. Score amount/attribution exactness, appropriate abstention, and correction count separately. A skipped or fallback provider run does not count as a successful AI run. Report every failure and sample size; do not infer general accuracy from the synthetic set.

## Merchant feasibility

Discuss with one real cabin operator: can separate sandbox buyers represent separate payments to one merchant, when can inventory be held, how long will the merchant honor a lease, who bears refund/fee costs, what happens on partial capture or failed inventory commit, and what support evidence is needed? Record name/role only with permission, date, direct observations, objections and unresolved questions. Separate hypothetical willingness from any actual agreement.

Blank record: `interview date | operator pseudonym/role | separate-payment constraints | inventory timing | recovery/fee responsibility | objections | next experiment | permission to quote`.
